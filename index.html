About

Notes to the accounts

The story behind the numbers — who I am, what drives me, and how I've spent the years leading up to my CA(SA) journey.

I'm a dedicated and ambitious accounting student working toward becoming a Chartered Accountant (SA). My passion for the discipline started in high school, where strong marks in accounting laid the foundation for the path I'm on now.

I'm highly motivated, detail-oriented, and committed to mastering the principles of accounting and finance — not just for exams, but for the kind of judgement the profession asks of you. Outside the classroom, I've coordinated finance and administration for a foundation, tutored fellow students through difficult accounting concepts, and helped run the books for a small manufacturing business. Each has sharpened a different part of the same instinct: make the numbers tell the truth, clearly.

  • ✓ Detail-oriented, deadline-driven, and comfortable with SARS-facing documentation
  • ✓ Experienced explaining complex accounting concepts in plain terms
  • ✓ Hands-on with budgeting, reconciliations and financial reporting
